Checking WebMCP

Your capabilities. Your agent. Your control.

WebMCP lets a website hand capabilities to an agent. Hitch explores the inverse: you bring capabilities you already own. Discover them over MCP, grant only the ones you choose, watch every call, and revoke instantly, without the page ever receiving the credentials behind them.
